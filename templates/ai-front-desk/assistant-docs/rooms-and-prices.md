# Rooms and prices

## Dune Room
Queen bed, garden view, rain shower, up to 2 guests. From €120 a night in low season (November to March) and €160 in high season (June to September). Dogs allowed.

## Ocean Room
King bed, private balcony over the bay, bathtub, up to 2 guests. From €165 a night in low season and €220 in high season.

## Family Loft
Two bedrooms under the roof (one double, two singles), up to 4 guests. From €210 a night in low season and €290 in high season.

## Booking and cancellation
Minimum stay is two nights in July and August. Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival; after that the first night is charged. We accept cards and bank transfer. The tourist tax of €2 per adult per night is paid at the house.
