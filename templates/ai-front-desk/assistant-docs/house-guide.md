# Maré House guide

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in is from 3pm to 10pm. If you arrive later, tell us in advance and we leave a key box code by email. Check-out is by 11am. You can leave luggage at reception after check-out for free until 6pm.

## Breakfast
Breakfast is included in every room rate. It is served on the terrace from 8:00 to 10:30, or in the lounge when it rains. We serve fresh bread from the village bakery, fruit, eggs any style, yoghurt, granola and pastéis de nata. Vegan and gluten-free options are available if you tell us the day before.

## Parking
There is no private parking at the house. Free street parking is on Rua do Norte, a three-minute walk away. The paid car park Parque da Praia costs about €8 a day.

## Pets
Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Dune Room for €15 a night. Please tell us when you book. Dogs are not allowed in the breakfast area.

## Wi-Fi
Fibre Wi-Fi in every room. The network is MareHouse-Guest; the password is on the card in your room.

## Children
Children of all ages are welcome. The Family Loft sleeps four. Cots are free; tell us when you book.

## Surf lessons
We work with Ericeira Surf Club two minutes away. A two-hour group lesson costs €40 with board and wetsuit. Book at reception the day before.

## Getting here
From Lisbon airport it is 45 minutes by car. The Mafrense bus from Campo Grande in Lisbon stops 400 metres from the house, every hour.
