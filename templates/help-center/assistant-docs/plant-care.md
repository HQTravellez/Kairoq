# Plant care basics

## Watering
Most of our plants like to dry out between waterings. Push a finger 3 cm into the soil: water only when it is dry. In winter, water about half as often. Every plant comes with a care card that shows its watering level from 1 (rarely) to 3 (often).

## Light
Monstera, pothos and philodendron like bright light but not direct midday sun. Snake plants and ZZ plants cope with low light and are the best choice for rooms with small windows. Cacti and succulents need a south-facing window.

## Pets
Snake plants, pothos, monstera and ZZ plants are toxic to cats and dogs if eaten. Pet-safe plants in our shop are marked with a paw icon; they include calathea, parlour palm, spider plant and peperomia.

## Plant guarantee
Every plant has a 30-day guarantee. If it struggles in the first 30 days even with the care card followed, send us photos and we will help, and replace it if needed.
