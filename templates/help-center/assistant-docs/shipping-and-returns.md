# Shipping and returns

## Delivery times
We ship plants Monday to Wednesday only, so they never sit in a warehouse over the weekend. Orders placed by 1pm on those days leave the same day. Delivery in mainland UK takes 1–2 working days. We do not ship outside the UK yet.

## Delivery cost
Standard delivery is £4.95. Delivery is free on orders over £50. Large plants (over 1 metre) cost £12 to deliver.

## Returns
If a plant arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we replace it or refund you. Pots and accessories can be returned unused within 30 days for a full refund. We cannot accept returns of healthy plants because they do not travel well twice.

## Order tracking
You get a tracking link by email when your order leaves. If it has not arrived after 3 working days, write to us and we will chase the courier.
