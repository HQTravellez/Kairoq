    How it works

    From a sentence to an app your customers can use.

    1. 1

      Describe it

      Say who it is for and what it does. Kairoq picks a design direction, writes the code and shows the preview as it streams in.

    2. 2

      Watch it get tested

      Every version opens in a real browser on a test copy of your data. Flows that work are saved as checks and re-run after each change.

    3. 3

      Publish and keep improving

      Go live on a Kairoq URL or your own domain. AI customers keep visiting, and each week you get a plain-English note on what is stopping signups.

    Pricing

    Credits, not seats. Pay for changes that work.

    How credits work

    • One message, one price. A build or a fix costs the credits of the model you pick. Discussing and visual edits are cheaper or free.
    • Our mistakes are on us. If a change opens with errors or breaks a saved check, the next fix of that problem costs nothing.
    • No loops. If the AI cannot fix something after a few tries, Kairoq stops and tells you instead of charging again.
    • Never expire. Credit packs stay until you use them. Subscriptions top up every month.

                                                Publish to the App Store and Google Play

                                                Kairoq prepares the listing, signs and builds the app on Expo's servers, and sends it to your store accounts. Nothing is sent to a store until you press a Build button.

                                                  1. Listing

                                                  Kairoq writes the text from your app, makes a 1024 px icon and takes iPhone and Android screenshots of your screens.

                                                  2. Apple

                                                  1. In App Store Connect, create the app: My Apps, then +, then New App, with the bundle ID .
                                                  2. Users and Access, then Integrations, then App Store Connect API: generate a key with the Admin role and download the .p8 file.
                                                  3. Paste the key here. Kairoq checks it with Apple, then makes the signing certificate and profile itself.

                                                  3. Google Play

                                                  1. In the Play Console, create the app.
                                                  2. The first Android build is uploaded by hand (Google's rule): Kairoq gives you the file.
                                                  3. For later builds, add a Google Cloud service account with access to the app and paste its JSON key here.

Kairoq films it in your site's colours (a pour, a splash, steam, the layers coming apart or a slow turn), puts it on your site as a scene that plays as visitors scroll, and makes a vertical reel to post.

                                                                                                      App Store and Google Play
                                                                                                      
    
                                                                                                      Turn on This is a mobile app, then:
                                                                                                      
    
                                                                                                        
      
                                                                                                      • Download the iOS + Android project (ZIP): open it in Android Studio or Xcode (npm run android / npm run ios).
                                                                                                        • 
      
                                                                                                      • Or push it to GitHub from Settings: every push builds an Android APK you can install, under Actions → Artifacts.
                                                                                                        • 
      
                                                                                                      • Publishing to the stores needs your own Apple Developer and Google Play accounts.

                                                                                                      Build from a reference
                                                                                                      
    
                                                                                                      Kairoq studies a product (its public pages, its code or screenshots), writes a plan for you to approve, then builds its own version: comparable features and design, with an original name, text and images. It follows robots.txt and never signs in or submits forms on the reference site.
