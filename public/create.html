    How it works

    From a sentence to an app your customers can use.

    1. 1

      Describe it

      Say who it is for and what it does. Kairoq picks a design direction, writes the code and shows the preview as it streams in.

    2. 2

      Watch it get tested

      Every version opens in a real browser on a test copy of your data. Flows that work are saved as checks and re-run after each change.

    3. 3

      Publish and keep improving

      Go live on a Kairoq URL or your own domain. AI customers keep visiting, and each week you get a plain-English note on what is stopping signups.

    How credits work

    • One message, one price. A build or a fix costs the credits of the model you pick. Discussing and visual edits are cheaper or free.
    • Our mistakes are on us. If a change opens with errors or breaks a saved check, the next fix of that problem costs nothing.
    • No loops. If the AI cannot fix something after a few tries, Kairoq stops and tells you instead of charging again.
    • Never expire. Credit packs stay until you use them. Subscriptions top up every month.
